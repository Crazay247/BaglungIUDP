    Plan at a glance

    Vision, goal and objectives

    Vision, goal and objectives that guide every sector plan and investment decision.

    Vision
    मध्यपहाडी व्यापारिक तथा पर्यटकीय शहर, सुन्दर, समृद्ध, सांस्कृतिक बागलुङ नगर

    A mid hill commercial and touristic city, a beautiful, prosperous and cultural Baglung. By 2036 the municipality is realised as a flood resilient, well connected gateway city of the Dhaulagiri region, anchored in trade, health, education and tourism, where every ward from the dense Ward 2 bazaar to the highlands of Wards 9 and 11 shares a dignified quality of life.

    Vision was drafted with municipal officials during the profile phase and tested in focus groups — deliberately short, in Nepali first as working language, English beside it.

    Grounded in three facts: Mid-Hill Highway & Kali Gandaki corridor location; Ward 2 holds 543/1,219 firms (44.5%, BCCI 2082 BS); Panchakot 5,000–7,000 visitors/day in season (field survey 2082 BS).

    Goal
    To build the foundation of a physically and economically advanced, socially inclusive, environmentally balanced and disaster and climate resilient municipality.

    The goal translates the vision into one sentence the budget can serve. Six objectives carry the goal into action — each measured by monitoring indicators. The objectives are deliberately few: a long list would dilute the budget the same way tukra yojana parcels dilute the capital.

    Six objectives

    Linked to sectors, projects and monitoring — objective → strategy → programme → project → investment → indicator.

    Objective 1Achieve durable, all weather physical connectivity to every ward and permanently resolve the recurring flood hazard of Baglung Bazaar.

    → Physical, DRR. Measured by monitoring indicators.

    Objective 2Diversify the local economy beyond retail trade through agro processing and heritage and adventure tourism.

    → Economic, Tourism.

    Objective 3Raise internal revenue and shared revenue autonomy from the current 29.2 percent toward long term fiscal self reliance.

    → Financial.

    Objective 4Strengthen health, education and social inclusion services so that quality does not depend on which ward a resident lives in.

    → Social.

    Objective 5Complete the zero waste system and reverse net forest loss in the urbanising core.

    → Environmental.

    Objective 6Build in house institutional and technical capacity in GIS and urban planning so that flagship projects are municipally led and not externally dependent.

    → Institutional.

    Each objective measured by monitoring indicators — see Monitoring & Scorecard.

    Principles that bind the plan

    Evidence first

    Every recommendation carries a data line and source.

    Spatial concentration

    Investment follows the bazaar core, corridor wards and river — not thin across 14 wards.

    GESI as a duty

    LG Operation Act 2074 §24 — lens of 46.4% female-headed households and ageing wards.

    Climate & disaster proofing

    Every physical project screened against flood and landslide risk.

    Fiscal honesty

    MSIP only commits what municipality can fund or realistically mobilise from partners.
