    Three Baglungs — the town, the corridor, the marginal hills

    The Social Development Plan is built on a "Three Baglungs" reading: a denser service-rich core, a thinning middle corridor, and hill wards (7, 9, 11) losing youth and ageing fast. Deprivation is not uniform — it is spatial.

    School rationalisation · Objectives 05.2

    Nine schools merge so every classroom meets standard

    The municipality runs many under-enrolled schools. The plan consolidates them into destination schools so student–teacher ratios land at 7–35:1 and every child has a viable school within reach.
