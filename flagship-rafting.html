Kali Gandaki Rafting Corridor

Concept, location, cost, delivery and financial appraisal of the flagship rafting investment.

📁 tourism🆔 flagship-01-rafting
Project detail

Concept & strategic rationale

The corridor runs ~25 km of the Kali Gandaki from the Balewa put-in near Ward 13 to the Ramrekha take-out near Ward 1 — grade 2–3 water through the gorge, with both access points placed above the modelled flood line. The bundle: river infrastructure (put-in / take-out ramps), a 12-raft fleet, a guide-training line (40 guides, Nepal Rafting Association standard), a booking · river-gauge · flood-stop system, and the shuttle link to the corridor homestay line.

Location
Where

Kali Gandaki gorge — Balewa (Ward 13) put-in → Ramrekha (Ward 1) take-out; ~25 km, grade 2–3; ramps above the flood line.
