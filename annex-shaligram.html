Concept & strategic rationale

The Shaligram Museum at Kundule is already a demand-backed tourism and pilgrimage anchor of Baglung. The working paper recommends focusing investment on visitor experience, interpretation, dwell time and services rather than creating an entirely new attraction — a meditation/wellness component, local-product/café spaces and improved pilgrim facilities, making Shaligram an economic catalyst for Ward 4.
