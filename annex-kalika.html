Concept & strategic rationale

The circuit links Kalika Bhagawati — one of the region’s important Shakti Peeths, Ward 1 — the Malika community forest (180 ha green anchor, Ward 5) and the Panchakot ridge (Ward 6). The product is a pedestrian precinct, heritage lighting, shopfront regulation, a ticket pool for the temple and adjacent sites, and the trail link to the Golden Bridge; heritage zone within 50 m of the temple and 100 m of the bridge anchor.
