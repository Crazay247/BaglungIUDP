    Seventy percent federal money
    is a risk, not a fact of life.

    Baglung's budget grew in Federations but 70.8% still arrives as central grants. The plan lifts own-source revenue through a Revenue DB & GIS tax layer, an asset inventory, and the one mispriced asset it already owns — the Bus Park.

    💰 FY82/83: 130.96 → 114.95 cr 📈 Autonomy 23.6% → 29.2% 📋 1,040 cr MSIP working envelope

    A budget that shrank while autonomy rose

    FY82/83 to FY83/84: total budget fell 12.2% from NPR 130.96 cr to 114.95 cr — federal grants dropped from 91.35 to 78.22 while internal & bank revenue rose 14.79 → 18.00. Autonomy climbed 23.6% → 29.2%, but the base is too thin.

    Strategy & programmes · RIAP T7 + Plans T11

    Revenue DB, asset inventory, one real bus park

    Nine RIAP actions and eight Plan actions operationalise the fiscal objective. The highest-leverage start: the Revenue Database with GIS tax layer — Phase I — High.

    T7 · RIAPRevenue DB + GIS tax

    Property, land and business tax unified on one register — the 29.2% autonomy flywheel.

    AssetsAsset & Bus Park revenue

    Inventory municipal land, buildings and the park; monetise what's under-used.

    MSIPEnvelope budgeting

    Pool 1,040 cr (25/55/17/3 public/PPP/grant/sources) against the programme phasing.

    PPP facilityPartnership framework

    A standing PPP structure to absorb the 55% private share of the envelope.

    Money note. Of the 1,040 cr envelope, total public outlay is 51.25 cr across P1–P5 — the crowding-in engine, not the whole bill. Risks: grant cut, TDF debt service. Source: Finance doc T7 / T11 / T14; cross-ref MSIP & Phasing.
