01 — Goal & Strategic Priority

Goal: A flood-resilient, connected and serviced municipality — pool NPR 6.40cr (ward allocations) + 4.00cr (municipal pool) for corridor projects; sequence drainage + road (drainage first, then blacktop) and standardise ROW. Source: v3 Ch5.1 Goal, objectives and strategy (3 moves) > For addition Ch5.1 LFA row.

Strategic priority: Flood-proof the bazaar + connect Wards 9,10,11 to blacktop, then standardise. Links to Objective 1 (all-weather connectivity + flood resolve).

02 — Existing Situation

The physical sector carries the heaviest gaps and the clearest geography. The bazaar core (Wards 1–4) runs on 5–8 m rights of way against 10–30 m standards (Nepal Urban Road Standard, 2011); the bazaar floods every monsoon from hillside runoff; only 13% of 322 km road network is blacktopped. Source: For addition Ch5.1.1 + v3 Ch5 Present condition.

  • 88.7% piped water, but Ward12 1002 HH unimproved, Wards9-11 no pucca; 47.1 km blacktop added last 4 yrs (NUGIP 12 km); Davgara Bus Park TDF 9cr debt; tukra yojana fragmentation.
  • Schools: 154, but many under-enrolled; drainage: bazaar storm trunk missing.

Provenance: For addition Ch5.1.1 + v3 Ch5 Present + Table 5.1 SWOT + Appendix A.2 DUDBC norms.

03 — Key Problems

Problem tree (v3 Fig5.1): Core problem — bazaar floods + crashes. Causes: narrow ROW, no trunk drains, earthen roads, unregulated building. Effects: 59 crashes /44 fatalities (74.6% fatality rate), ponding, dust, access loss. Source: v3 Ch5 Problem analysis Fig5.1 (4 causes → core →4 effects); For addition Ch5.1.3 image tree same.

04 — Demand / Gap

Demand against DUDBC Annex A.2: ROW 10-30m vs 5-8m actual; road: need ~64.2 km strategic blacktop (see Programmes); water: demand 6.9 MLD vs capacity 5 MLD (120 lpcd); sanitation/drainage/waste gaps in Ch4.3. Ward road priority Table 5.5 and Ward water demand Table 5.8 at 120 lpcd. Source: v3 Ch4.3 + Annex A.2 + For addition Ch4.3.

05 — Objectives

Objective tree (v3 Fig5.2): Means: 12 km trunk drains +15 km catch drains; 60 km blacktop; 4×700kL tanks; bus park operationalised. Ends: flood elimination + all-ward motorable network. Source: For addition Ch5.1.4 / v3 Ch5 Objective analysis.
