Protect your privacy · आफ्नो गोपनीयता जोगाउनुहोस्

If someone else may use this phone or computer, these small habits make a big difference.

  • Quick Exit · तुरुन्तै बाहिरिने — the corner button (or press Esc twice) instantly replaces this page with a harmless one and removes it from your back-button history.
  • Leave fewer traces · पदचिन्ह घटाउनुहोस् — open this page in an Incognito / Private window, or clear your browser history afterwards. If your device may be monitored, file your report without a name or phone number.
  • In danger now? · अहिले खतरा भए — call 100 or 1145 (free, 24/7). Move to a safe place, and if you were hurt, seek medical care as soon as possible — Dhaulagiri Hospital: 068-520288.
  • Helping someone else · अरूलाई सहयोग — you may report on behalf of another person. Listen without judging, let her decide what comes next, and offer to call 1145 together or visit the police Women's Cell with her.
  • After a report · उजुरीपछि के हुन्छ — here, reports stay in your browser only. In the full municipal service, a trained woman officer would review it confidentially and connect you with counselling, legal aid or protection — following the National Women Commission's "Khabar Garaun" confidentiality rules.
Empowerment · महिला सशक्तीकरण

Know your rights · आफ्नो अधिकार जान्नुहोस्

These are general information, not legal advice. For your specific case, free legal aid is available — see the helplines below.

  • Constitution, Article 38 · संविधान — every woman has the right to live with dignity, equal rights in ancestral property, safe motherhood, and freedom from all forms of violence, discrimination and untouchability — at home, in public, and at work.
  • Domestic Violence Act, 2066 · घरेलु हिंसा ऐन — physical, mental, sexual and economic harm by a family or household member is a crime. Protection orders, compensation and safe accommodation are available through the police, ward office or court.
  • Muluki Criminal Code, 2074 · मुलुकी अपराध संहिता — rape (including marital rape), sexual harassment, child marriage, dowry demands, witchcraft accusations, polygamy and acid attacks are all crimes — reported free, handled confidentially.
  • Free legal aid · निःशुल्क कानुनी सहायता — the National Women Commission (1145), police Women's Cells (महिला सहायता कक्ष), and the District Legal Aid Committee provide free lawyers and confidential counselling to those who cannot afford them.
  • Empowerment basics · सशक्तीकरण — register citizenship and property in your own name; daughters and sons inherit equally under the Civil Code 2074; secondary education in community schools is free for girls and boys alike.

⚖️ General information only — not legal advice. Read more at nwchelpline.gov.np (Khabar Garaun) or baglungmun.gov.np.

Questions · प्रश्नहरू

Good to know · जान्नुपर्ने कुराहरू

Is my report sent to anyone?
No. This is a demonstration prototype: reports stay in your own browser only and are never transmitted to the municipality, police, or anyone else. If you need real help today, call 100 or 1145 — both are free.
Will anyone know I visited this page?
This page appears in your browser history like any website. Use the Quick Exit button to leave instantly, and open it in an Incognito/Private window (or clear your history afterwards) if your device is shared. The report itself is anonymous unless you choose to add your name.
Can I report something that happened to someone else?
Yes. Family members, neighbours and witnesses can report. If it is safe, talk to the survivor first and involve her in the decision — her consent and safety come first.
What if I have no evidence or proof?
You do not need proof to be heard, to call a helpline, or to seek advice. Real helplines and the police Women's Cell are trained to receive complaints without evidence and will guide you on what happens next. Do not delay medical care if you were hurt — treatment also preserves evidence.
Why do real helplines exist alongside this demo?
Because your safety matters more than any prototype. 1145 (Khabar Garaun) is the National Women Commission's free 24/7 helpline, and 100 reaches the police from any phone, free of charge. In Baglung, the District Police Office (068-520199) has a Women's Cell, and Dhaulagiri Hospital (068-520288) treats injuries confidentially.
Why does this page exist on an urban planning site?
The Baglung IUDP treats women's safety as urban development: lighting, transport, health services, and public spaces all shape whether women can move, work and live freely. This portal is an academic prototype exploring how the municipality could one day receive such reports online — especially for women in far wards. It was designed with safety-first UX guidance (always-visible helplines, quick-exit, anonymity).
