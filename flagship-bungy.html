Kali Gandaki Gorge Bungy

Concept, location, cost, delivery and appraisal of the flagship adventure investment.

📁 tourism🆔 flagship-02-bungy
Project detail

Concept & strategic rationale

The bungy site sits on the Baglung bank of the Kali Gandaki gorge opposite the Kushma jump, in the Ramrekha–Maldhunga belt of Ward 1. The gorge wall offers a 120–150 m free drop, the structure is anchored to the bedrock line the DEM shows at the gorge edge, and the corridor road and Kalika precinct trail already serve the site.

Location
Where

Ward 1 — Ramrekha–Maldhunga belt, Baglung bank opposite Kushma (Parbat); 120–150 m free drop; landslide and fault risk excluded the unstable benches.
