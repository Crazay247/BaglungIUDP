    From retail town
    to value-adding economy.

    2,907 establishments run on 9,320 jobs, but 52.9% are trade. The core problem is not volume — it is value. The plan adds the missing cold chain, collection and branding so Baglung's produce leaves as product, not raw.

    Existing situation

    A trade economy, concentrated in the core

    Baglung's economy is real but shallow: establishment lifetimes, trade dominance and retail concentration in Ward 2 describe a service economy with little manufacturing or agro-processing.

    The problem

    Low value addition is the core

    The problem tree converges on one core: local produce and enterprise capture almost none of the value chain. Three root causes stand out — and each has a matching solution output.

    Root causeConsequenceObjective output
    No cold chainPerishables sold raw / spoil in transitCold chain 500–1,000 T (Wards 8 / 4)
    No collection systemFarmers individually carry tiny volumesCollection & aggregation network
    No brandingNo "Made in Baglung" premiumBranding of 15 signature products
    Objectives

    Six outputs that lift the value chain

    The objective tree converts the three root causes into six measurable outputs — cold chain, collection, branding, plus processing, market linkage and local-enterprise support.

    What we'll build

    A cold chain, a collection net, and a Made-in-Baglung shelf

    P1 Agro · 33 crCold chain Ward 8 / 4

    500–1,000 T capacity — modular PPP/cooperative build (18 + 10 + 5 phased), serving the plantation belt.

    CollectionAggregation network

    Farm-level collection nodes feed the cold chain and the processing units.

    BrandingMade-in-Baglung · 15 products

    Signature agro products branded and marketed off the Dhaulagiri craft.

    Heritage circuitCulture-linked enterprise

    Heritage circuit and dairy/meat programmes tie the economy to tourism and livestock wards.

    SpatialAgro cluster · Ward 6

    Orange (1 cr crop) cluster anchors the plantation map in the corridor — see Spatial Plan.
