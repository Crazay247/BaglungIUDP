    Panchakot Cable Car

    Concept, location, cost, delivery and appraisal of the flagship tourism investment.

    📁 tourism🆔 flagship-03-cablecar
    Project detail

    Concept & strategic rationale

    Panchakot draws 5,000–7,000 visitors/day in season on the current road and foot access; the cable car converts that demand into reliable access and revenue. About 1.6 km from the bazaar edge in Ward 4 to the Panchakot ridge in Ward 6, lifting ~700 m along the ridge shoulder, with two stations, 60 passenger cabins (DoT + civil aviation standard) and ridge development — viewpoints, temple precinct upgrade, food and craft outlets.

    Location
    Where

    Ward 4 lower station on municipal land beside the corridor road → Ward 6 Panchakot ridge; ~1.6 km, ~700 m lift; alignment follows suitability and slope analysis.

    Cost & phasing

    Components / sub-projects

    Components: ropeway line + stations, 1.6 km (NPR 52.00 cr) · ridge development, viewpoints + precinct (8.00) · feeder road + parking (5.00) · operator setup + certification (3.00) · environmental + landscape works (2.00) — total NPR 70.00 cr (VGF 20.00 + municipal in-kind 5.00 + operator 45.00).
