Headline findings

Baglung is not declining — it is changing

Older, smaller, more female-headed, and richer per household. The plan trades falling school capacity for rising elder care, builds services for women-led households, and holds the hills by rebuilding where the deprivation index sits highest.

Ageing60+ rises to ~20%

The 60+ share climbs from 13.2% (2021) toward one in five by 2031 — old-age dependency nearly doubles.

Households16,187 by 2031

People fall, yet households keep rising as family size drops from 3.81 to 3.52 — new homes are still needed.

Youth drain14.4% abroad

One in seven residents is abroad; 80.7% of leavers are aged 15–34. Return-migration is the labour opportunity.

The core pull53% on 16% of land

Wards 1–4 & 14 concentrate over half the people on a fraction of the area — up to a 70:1 density spread.
