Concept & strategic rationale

Ward 10 holds 11 registered homestays but only 4 operate, while Baraha Lake draws 150–300 visitors/day in peak. The product is a homestay revival programme, trail signage, a central booking desk and a quality standard, linked to the orange orchards and ridge walks — Sirubari and Ghalegaun reach 60–80% occupancy in season when the booking system works.
